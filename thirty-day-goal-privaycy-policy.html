Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the 30 Day Challenge Android app (package name com.habitspark.thirtydaychallenge), published by KOGEI (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information when you use it.

30 Day Challenge lets you start ready-made or custom 30-day challenges, check in once a day with an optional note, and follow your progress through a 30-day calendar, history and insights such as streaks and completion rates.

In short: the app works fully offline. Everything you enter stays on your device, and we do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your information.

Information We Collect

Personal information

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no sign-up or login, and it never asks for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details.

Device and usage information

We do not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, usage statistics or crash reports. The app does not connect to the internet and has no servers, so no information is sent to us.

Data Stored on Your Device

To work, the app saves the following on your device only, in its private app storage:

Data the app stores locally
DataWhat it includesWhy
ChallengesTitle, optional description, chosen icon and color, start and end dates, and status (active, completed, finished or abandoned)To show your challenges and their progress
Daily check-insWhich of the 30 days you checked in, when you checked in, and any optional note you write (up to 200 characters)To build your calendar, streaks, history and insights
App preferenceA single flag recording that the app has been opened beforeTo handle the first launch

Please avoid writing sensitive personal information in challenge titles, descriptions or notes. Even though this data stays on your device, it is stored as you type it.

How Information Is Used

The data described above is used only inside the app, on your device, to:

  • Display your active challenges and today's check-in status
  • Show your 30-day calendar, history and past challenges
  • Calculate insights such as streaks, completion rates and recent activity

It is never used for advertising, profiling or any purpose outside the app.

App Permissions

The app does not request any permissions. In particular, it does not use:

  • Internet access
  • Location
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Contacts
  • Photos & files
  • Notifications

Third-Party Services

The app does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, authentication, payment, cloud or AI services, and it does not use Firebase. It is built only with Google's standard Android libraries (AndroidX, Material Components and the Room database), which run on your device and do not send data anywhere.

Links that leave the app

  • Rate Us opens the app's page in the Google Play Store app (or in your browser if the Play Store is not available).
  • Share App opens your device's share menu with a short message and a link to the app on Google Play. You choose whether and where to share it.

Once you leave the app, the Google Play Store, your browser or the app you share with handles your information under its own privacy policy. For Google, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share any information with third parties, because we do not collect any. Your challenges and check-ins never leave your device through the app.

Device Backup & Transfer

So that you can keep your progress when you change phones, the app allows Android to include its local data (your challenges, check-ins and the app preference) in:

  • Android backup, if you have backup turned on in your device settings
  • Device-to-device transfer, when you set up a new phone

These backups are created and stored by Android and your Google account, not by us, and we cannot access them. You can turn backup on or off at any time in your device's system settings.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time:

  • A single abandoned challenge: open History and tap the delete icon on the challenge. It and all its check-ins are permanently removed.
  • All app data: clear the app's storage in your device settings (Settings → Apps → 30 Day Challenge → Storage → Clear storage).
  • Everything: uninstall the app. This removes all of its data from the device.

Copies held in your Android backup are kept and deleted according to your Google account's backup settings.

Data Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android's app sandbox prevents other apps from reading. Because the app never transmits your data, there is no risk of it being intercepted in transit or exposed through our servers. Keeping your device protected with a screen lock adds a further layer of protection.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the relevant age in your country). Since no information is collected or sent to us, no data from children is ever received.

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, privacy laws (such as the GDPR or CCPA) may give you rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data. Because we do not collect or hold any of your data, there is nothing for us to provide, change or delete on our side. You stay in full control of the data on your device and can view, edit or delete it using the app or your device settings, as described above.

If you have questions about your privacy, you can always contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the app gains new features. When we do, we will publish the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above. If a change affects how your information is handled, we will also reflect it in the app before it takes effect.
